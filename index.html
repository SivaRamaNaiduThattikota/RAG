Concept 05 opens probability.

Concepts 01 through 04 closed this module's geometry thread—a vector's own norm, then two vectors compared by direction and by distance. Concept 05 leaves that thread behind and opens probability from a clean start: a discrete probability distribution assigns every outcome a number so the whole set sums to exactly 1; conditional probability P(A|B) asks how likely A is once B is already known; and Bayes' rule, derived here from nothing but that definition, is what lets a twenty-ticket example update a 25% base rate to about 57% once one keyword is observed. Return with questions or your teach-back before we mark it complete.
