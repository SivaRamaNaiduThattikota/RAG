Concept 07 active.
Document-length normalization.

Concept 06 wrote BM25's length-normalization parameter, b, straight into the formula, then held every document's length equal to the corpus average so b never once changed a score. Concept 07 removes that simplification and derives the length-adjustment term L(d) = 1 − b + b × dl/avgdl, working a complete example that contrasts a genuinely short and a genuinely long document from the course's own five-ticket corpus. Return with questions or your teach-back before we open Concept 08, Precision, recall and F-score.
