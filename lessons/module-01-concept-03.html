01 · CONCEPT FIRST

What is a generative model family?

A generative model family is a broad strategy for learning a data distribution and producing new samples. The families share the generative goal from Concept 2, but they reach it through different training signals and generation procedures.

Think of several routes to the same destination. One model generates the next piece after the pieces already produced. Another compresses an example into a latent code and decodes a nearby code. Another learns through competition. Another starts with noise and repeatedly removes it.

First memory: “generative” describes the goal; autoregressive, VAE, GAN, diffusion, flow, and energy-based describe different mechanisms.
02 · WHY IT EXISTS

Why are there several families?

Generative modelling asks a difficult question: how can a machine represent a complicated distribution containing language, images, audio, code, or other high-dimensional data? Different families make different parts of that problem easier.

ONE FAMILY MAY PRIORITIZE

An explicit probability for every sample, stable likelihood-based training, or a useful compressed representation.

ANOTHER MAY PRIORITIZE

Sharp-looking samples, parallel generation, flexible conditioning, or a simpler sampling process.

Those priorities conflict. Autoregressive generation is naturally sequential. GAN sampling can be fast, but training can be unstable. Diffusion training is comparatively stable, but sampling traditionally requires repeated steps. Normalizing flows give tractable densities, but invertibility constrains their design.

Selection principle: choose by data type, likelihood requirement, sample quality, latency, controllability, training stability, and deployment cost—not popularity.
03 · CONCRETE EXAMPLE

Generate one employee-policy answer in different ways

Continuity from Concepts 1 and 2: retrieval supplies policy evidence, Oracle supplies exact balances, and generation expresses the verified result. This lesson asks which kind of generator could produce that expression.

An autoregressive language model can produce the answer one token at a time: “Your” → “remaining” → “leave” → “balance” → …. Each next token depends on the question, retrieved policy, database result, and tokens already generated.

Other families are useful for different jobs. A VAE may learn a compact latent representation of document layouts. A GAN may generate synthetic images or test data. A diffusion model may create or edit images. A flow may be used when exact density evaluation matters. None of these should invent an employee’s real balance.

RAG ANSWER

Usually autoregressive text generation conditioned on retrieved evidence.

SYNTHETIC DATA

May use VAE, GAN, diffusion, flow, or a hybrid depending on the modality.

AUTHORITATIVE FACT

Still comes from retrieval, Oracle, or a deterministic tool—not the family itself.

04 · TECHNICAL MECHANISM

The six major families in plain language

AUTOREGRESSIVE

Continue the sequence

Factor data into an ordered sequence and predict each next element from earlier elements.

LATENT VARIABLE · VAE

Encode, sample, decode

Learn a structured latent space and reconstruct or generate data from sampled latent codes.

ADVERSARIAL · GAN

Generator versus critic

Train a generator to produce samples that a discriminator cannot distinguish from real data.

DIFFUSION / SCORE

Noise, then denoise

Learn to reverse a gradual corruption process and turn noise into a structured sample.

NORMALIZING FLOW

Invertible transformation

Transform a simple distribution into a complex one through reversible mappings.

ENERGY-BASED

Score compatibility

Assign low energy to compatible configurations and high energy to unlikely ones; sampling finds low-energy regions.

Hybrids combine mechanisms—for example, latent diffusion uses an encoder-decoder latent space with diffusion inside that space.

06 · IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Do not confuse family, architecture, and modality

Family versus neural-network architecture

“Diffusion” describes a generative process and objective. A U-Net or Transformer may implement its denoiser. “Autoregressive” describes a probability factorization; a Transformer, recurrent network, or convolutional network may implement it.

Family versus modality

Text does not automatically mean autoregressive, and image does not automatically mean diffusion. These are common pairings, not definitions. Autoregressive models have generated images and audio; diffusion models can operate on several modalities.

Autoencoder versus VAE

A plain autoencoder learns reconstruction. A VAE adds a probabilistic latent model and regularization that supports sampling from a meaningful latent distribution.

GAN discriminator versus discriminative model

The GAN discriminator performs a discriminative training job, but its feedback trains a generative system. This extends Concept 2 rather than contradicting it.

Preview boundary: modern LLMs are commonly autoregressive Transformer models, but Transformers, foundation models, and LLMs belong to the next lesson.
07 · COMMON MISTAKES

What beginners often misunderstand

“An LLM is a separate seventh family.”

Most decoder-style LLMs use autoregressive language modelling. “LLM” describes scale, domain, and capability; “autoregressive” describes the generation factorization.

“GAN means any AI-generated image.”

GAN is one adversarial training family. Many modern image generators use diffusion or hybrid approaches.

“A VAE is just a compressed file format.”

Its latent variables are learned probabilistically; the objective balances reconstruction with latent-distribution regularization.

“Diffusion retrieves a cleaner training image.”

It learns a denoising process and synthesizes a new sample; it is not document retrieval.

“Exact likelihood guarantees realistic output.”

Probability tractability, perceptual quality, diversity, and usefulness are different evaluation dimensions.

“One family must replace all others.”

Hybrids deliberately combine complementary mechanisms.

08 · YOUR PROJECT MAPPING

Where model families appear in Oracle RAG

FINAL ANSWER · AUTOREGRESSIVE Generate grounded text

The language model emits tokens conditioned on the question, retrieved passages, and exact Oracle values.

EMBEDDINGS · NOT THIS JOB Represent for retrieval

An embedding model maps text to vectors. Its role in the RAG runtime is representation and similarity, not free-form answer generation.

ORACLE · DETERMINISTIC Return authoritative records

The database executes validated SQL. No generative family should guess the balance.

SYNTHETIC TESTING · OPTIONAL Create non-production examples

A generative family may create privacy-safe-looking test documents, but generated data must never be presented as a real record.

User question → Route + authorization → Oracle lookup + policy retrieval → Verified context → Autoregressive generator → Grounded answer

LangChain or LangGraph may orchestrate this flow later, but neither is a generative model family. They coordinate models, retrieval, tools, state, and control logic.

DEPENDENCIES

Update parameters
Validate quality and coverage
GENERATION

09
MATHEMATICS

The signature equation of each family

Let x=(x₁,…,xₙ) be data, z latent noise, c a condition, θ model parameters, and φ inference parameters.

Autoregressive: p(x) = ∏ᵢ p(xᵢ | x₁,…,xᵢ₋₁)
VAE ELBO: log pθ(x) ≥ Eqφ(z|x)[log pθ(x|z)] − KL(qφ(z|x) || p(z))
GAN: minG maxD Ex~pdata[log D(x)] + Ez~p(z)[log(1−D(G(z)))]
Diffusion noise objective: Ex₀,t,ε[ || ε − εθ(xₜ,t,c) ||² ]
Flow: log pX(x) = log pZ(f⁻¹(x)) + log |det Jf⁻¹(x)|
Energy model: pθ(x) = exp(−Eθ(x)) / Zθ

Each equation exposes what the family makes tractable and where approximation or difficult sampling enters.

10
WORKED EXAMPLE

Autoregressive probability of a tiny answer

Suppose a toy model generates three tokens: “leave”, “balance”, “available”. It assigns:

Conditional event Probability
p(leave | start, context) 0.50
p(balance | leave, context) 0.40
p(available | leave balance, context) 0.25
p(sequence | context) = 0.50 × 0.40 × 0.25 = 0.05

The 5% is the probability of that exact sequence under this toy model. Real language models compare vast token vocabularies at every position. This calculation explains factorization, not LLM architecture.

12
CODE

From-scratch autoregressive sampler

PYTHON · TOY MECHANISM 
import random

next_token = {
    "<start>": {"Your": 0.7, "The": 0.3},
    "Your": {"balance": 0.8, "policy": 0.2},
    "balance": {"is": 1.0},
    "is": {"available": 0.6, "verified": 0.4},
}

def sample(options):
    words, weights = zip(*options.items())
    return random.choices(words, weights=weights, k=1)[0]

token = "<start>"
result = []
while token in next_token and len(result) < 4:
    token = sample(next_token[token])
    result.append(token)

print(" ".join(result))

This is a tiny Markov-style model, not an LLM. It makes the defining autoregressive loop visible: use the existing prefix, produce one next item, append it, and repeat.

13
ECOSYSTEM

One library-level categorical step

PYTHON · PYTORCH 
import torch
from torch.distributions import Categorical

logits = torch.tensor([2.0, 1.0, 0.2])
distribution = Categorical(logits=logits)
next_token_id = distribution.sample()
log_probability = distribution.log_prob(next_token_id)

print(next_token_id.item(), log_probability.item())

A real autoregressive model computes new logits from the full prefix and context at every step. VAEs, GANs, diffusion models, and flows use different training loops and samplers; hiding all of them behind one library call would obscure this lesson’s comparison.

16
DIAGNOSIS

Family-specific failure modes

AUTOREGRESSIVE

Exposure and compounding errors

An early bad choice becomes part of the later prefix.

VAE

Posterior collapse

A powerful decoder ignores the latent variable.

GAN

Mode collapse

The generator covers only a narrow part of the data distribution.

DIFFUSION

Slow or over-guided sampling

Too many steps cost time; excessive guidance can reduce diversity.

FLOW

Inflexible transforms

Invertibility and Jacobian constraints limit architecture choices.

ENERGY

Poor mixing

The sampler may not explore separated low-energy regions.

17
CORRECTION

Misconceptions

Incorrect belief Correction
Transformer is a generative family Transformer is an architecture; it can implement autoregressive or other objectives.
All autoencoders are VAEs A VAE adds probabilistic latent inference and an ELBO-style objective.
GAN discriminators retrieve evidence They distinguish generated from real training samples; retrieval is a separate search process.
Diffusion means adding noise at inference only Training defines a corruption/denoising problem; generation follows a learned reverse process.
Hybrids are taxonomy mistakes They intentionally combine complementary mechanisms.
18
REALITY

Production implications

  • Profile end-to-end sampling latency, not only one network pass.
  • Measure sample quality, diversity, factuality, likelihood, and task utility separately.
  • Version the generator, sampler, guidance settings, random seed policy, and evaluation set.
  • Treat generated synthetic data as synthetic and prevent it from contaminating authoritative records.
  • Monitor mode collapse, repetition, drift, memorization, privacy leakage, and unsafe conditioning.
  • For RAG, evaluate retrieval and generation separately before diagnosing the model family.
19
RECALL

Knowledge check

Which family factorizes a joint distribution into ordered conditionals?
Autoregressive models.
Why can a GAN sample without an explicit likelihood?
The generator defines an implicit distribution by transforming noise; the discriminator supplies the training signal.
What makes a normalizing flow tractable?
Invertible transformations with computable Jacobian determinants support change-of-variables density evaluation.
What is the VAE’s two-part objective?
Expected reconstruction log-likelihood plus a KL term that regularizes the approximate posterior toward the prior.
Why can diffusion sampling be expensive?
Generation commonly applies many reverse or refinement steps.
Does RAG require a diffusion model?
No. Text RAG commonly uses an autoregressive LLM, while retrieval remains a separate subsystem.

FEEDBACK

Solution and teach-back

Open one defensible solution
  1. Autoregressive text model; sequential latency and hallucination risk; not authoritative without evidence.
  2. GAN or diffusion; compare one-pass sampling with coverage/stability versus iterative cost; always synthetic.
  3. VAE; useful latent structure but reconstruction/latent-collapse trade-offs; synthetic representation.
  4. Normalizing flow; invertibility restricts design; density is model-derived, not a business record.
  5. Diffusion; high compute and sampling latency; synthetic.
  6. No generative family. Use authorized Oracle lookup and deterministic calculation.

23
CAREER PRACTICE

Interview questions

Compare autoregressive and diffusion sampling.
Autoregressive models extend an ordered prefix one element at a time; diffusion models iteratively refine a noisy state. Both may be sequential, but the state and objective differ.
Why does a VAE use the reparameterization trick?
It expresses stochastic latent sampling as a differentiable transformation of noise, enabling gradient-based optimization of encoder parameters.
Why can GANs suffer mode collapse?
The generator may find a limited set of outputs that successfully fools the discriminator instead of covering the full data distribution.
What is the price of exact likelihood in flows?
Transforms must be invertible with tractable Jacobian determinants, which constrains architecture design.
How do hybrids break simple labels?
A system may combine a VAE-style latent space, diffusion denoising, and Transformer components; classification should name each mechanism and role.
Which family is the RAG retriever?
None by default. Retrieval is search over external knowledge; the answer generator is often autoregressive.
